This is our testbed for lighting and shadow mapping techniques.

Here we demonstrate the engine's support for point and spot lights. 

Three colored point lights rotate around the model, and a white spot light from above swings back and forth.

All materials use the built-in PBR metallic-roughness lighting model.

Directional lights are also supported by the engine, but were skipped for this demo. I wanted the drama.

## Assets

The floor and suzanne models were shoddily put together in Blender.
